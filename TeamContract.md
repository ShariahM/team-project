# Team Contract

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Instagram for communication outside of class, when required for work on your course project.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages in at most within 24h.

* Teammates should be transparent and notify each other about absences (lec + tut), delays to deadlines, and any concerns.

* Actively listening to each team members ideas, giving everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute, and providing constructive criticism.

---

## Decision Making

* Majority vote will determine decisions about our team project.

---
## Conflict resolution

* Do your best to prevent them in the first place

* Listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.

---

## Accountability

* Completing their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required.

---
